# Crew naming boundary

- The customer-facing product name is **Crew**.
- Existing repository, crate, binary, bundle-path, environment-variable, storage-key, protocol, and service identifiers may remain `Comet`/`comet` for compatibility.
- New or edited user-facing copy must say **Crew**. Do not expose `Comet` as the product name.
- Do not rename internal identifiers during unrelated work. An internal rename requires its own compatibility and migration plan.
